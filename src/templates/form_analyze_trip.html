Landmarks (cues, waypoints, etc) come from a RWGPS route. You can enter a full URL or just the identifying number. You can search (🔍) for your own routes or your pinned routes.

🔍 Route URL:

A route URL will look like https://ridewithgps.com/routes/123456789

A trip is the record of your ride. That is where we'll find the time you passed each landmark in the route. You can pick a recent ride using the search function (🔍).

🔍 Trip URL:

A trip URL will look like https://ridewithgps.com/trips/123456789

Waypoints and special cues on the route are always included.
