Time Planner trip and route

To match times to a route, both the route and trip must be in Ride With GPS and accessible to you. Open those pages in other browser tabs so you can copy their URLs to paste below.

A trip URL will look like https://ridewithgps.com/trips/123456789

A route URL will look like https://ridewithgps.com/routes/123456789
